About Me

I've always been the kind of person who wants to understand why something works, not just that it works. As a Computer Science student, I kept coming back to the same question: What story is the data telling?

Over time, that curiosity turned into hands-on experience with SQL, Python, R, and Excel — cleaning data, exploring patterns, tracking KPIs, and breaking down trends. I enjoy making complex information easier to understand, especially for people who don't come from a technical background.

I'm also interested in how AI fits into analytics — not as a buzzword, but as a tool that makes analysis more efficient and insights more actionable when used thoughtfully.
